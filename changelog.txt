= Unreleased =
* Optional consent records: anonymous cookie ID, category/service choices, model, browser and receipt times, and policy label; admin-only CSV export, indexed per-site storage, bounded cron retention and uninstall cleanup. Off by default, with no ordinary page-view request or IP logging.
* Consent card: Scan now samples up to eight public same-origin pages with bounded loopback requests, updates findings in place and reports blocked requests; a three-step guide explains enabling consent, checking findings and adding the Cookie policy block.
* Cookie consent: hold back recognised scripts and iframes added after page load, including lightboxes, and update the banner and settings when they appear.
* Terms and conditions: off-by-default block and [spcc_terms] shortcode; business, country, offering, returns and contact answers; optional plain-text wording override; one-time import of unset Complianz Terms & Conditions identity/contact answers, leaving source options and pages unchanged.
* Local YouTube and Vimeo thumbnails in held-back video placeholders; WP-Cron downloads validated images once per video, retries failures after a day, and uninstall removes files, cache options and queued events.
* Cookie consent: holds back scripts and embeds from 23 services until visitors allow them; banner only on pages that have them; asks first by region (time zone); Global Privacy Control; Google Consent Mode v2; WP Consent API; embed placeholders with a Load button; Cookie settings dialog, footer menu link and button; imports Complianz's region and expiry settings once.
* Cookie policy block and shortcode that list the site's cookies and stay up to date; suggested privacy policy text.
* Settings screen: banner preview on the Consent card (asked-first or notice visitor, desktop or phone; updates after each change) and a page picker for the Cookie policy page.
* Blocks: Cookie policy block settings (introduction, Cookie settings button, last-updated date, heading level); Cookie settings block text and button or link style; the shortcodes take the same options.
* Cookie consent: the floating Cookie settings button is optional and off by default; the banner shows above floating language switchers and chat buttons; the dialog's close button and the banner buttons keep their colours on hover and focus in themes that style every button.
